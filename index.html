Tower Rush privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Tower Rush is a standalone arcade game. It does not require an account, collect a player name, offer chat or user posts, or sell in-app purchases.

Data stored by the game

The game stores your best height and preferences (sound, music/effects volume and tilt sensitivity) locally on this device. These are not sent to the developer. Optional tilt controls read motion/orientation only while enabled to steer the character. Sensor readings are neither recorded nor transmitted. Permission can be refused; touch controls remain available.

To clear saved progress and preferences, open Privacy and data inside the installed iOS game and choose Clear saved game data. This website cannot erase another app’s storage.

Advertising and consent

The iOS version may show occasional interstitial ads using Google Mobile Ads. The Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices where required. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized in the current build. The SDK may process data such as IP address, device identifiers, diagnostics and crash information, performance information, ad impressions, and interactions to provide, measure, and improve advertising. The exact data depends on the SDK version, region, consent, and final AdMob configuration.

You can reopen the available ad privacy choices from the game’s start or game-over screen. Google explains its data use in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and publishes the SDK’s current iOS App Store data disclosure.

The current build does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization and is configured for non-personalized ads. If the released SDK or ad settings are changed to track users across other companies’ apps or websites, the app and this policy must be updated before release.

Retention and deletion

The saved best height and game preferences can be removed with the button on this page. This does not erase Google advertising/consent records. Removing Tower Rush also removes its on-device game data. Advertising and consent records handled by Google are subject to Google’s controls and retention practices; use the privacy controls above and Google’s privacy resources to manage those choices.

Children’s privacy

The app does not ask for a player’s name, birth date, or contact details. The final age rating and audience must be declared accurately in App Store Connect. If the Kids Category is selected, review the advertising and data flows against Apple’s additional Kids Category requirements.
